# Sales & Revenue Centre
Master-only GET summary reads existing properties, OTA rate profiles, competitor reports, occupancy reports and revenue snapshots. No writes, live OTA calls, report generation or migrations. Existing workspaces remain available. Each source capped at 500 newest rows (properties alphabetical); counts and latest-per-property coverage can be incomplete. Missing source is distinct from no returned record. No cross-period occupancy average or mixed-currency revenue totals. Existing Lead/Onboarding/Active property statuses provide preliminary pipeline counts only. Listing quality and marketing sections are explicitly planned; no fabricated scores. Future listing audits should reuse property IDs and channel metadata; marketing needs source, campaign, period, spend currency and attribution; sales needs opportunity and activity records after schema review and approval. Test authorization, partial failures, caps, date ranges and navigation before relying on dashboard.
